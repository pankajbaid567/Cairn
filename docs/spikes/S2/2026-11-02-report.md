Fell back under RLS: fts (FTS index not used in 12 of 12 plans); fts_explicit_acl (FTS index not used in 12 of 12 plans); hnsw (HNSW index not used in 3 of 12 plans); hnsw_explicit_acl (HNSW index not used in 6 of 12 plans); hybrid_rrf (FTS index not used in 12 of 12 plans, HNSW index not used in 6 of 12 plans); hybrid_rrf_explicit_acl (FTS index not used in 12 of 12 plans, HNSW index not used in 9 of 12 plans). Default-deny checks: passed. Lexical search is native full-text search: the stored generated column `body_tsv` (`to_tsvector('simple', body)`) with a GIN index, `websearch_to_tsquery` and `ts_rank_cd`.
