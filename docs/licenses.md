## Policy

- Allowed for direct dependencies: Apache-2.0, BSD-2-Clause, BSD-3-Clause, ISC, MIT, MPL-2.0, PSF-2.0.
- `A AND B` needs both allowed; `A OR B` needs one of them allowed.
- Denied in `uv.lock` (decision D10): `celery`, `kombu`, `redis`.
- Transitive packages outside the allowlist are listed for review and never fail the run.
- Container images need an entry in the image table. AGPL-3.0 is accepted only for the Grafana images.
